# R78 — bilingual themed reward surfaces

R78 makes Gift Road, Pasha benefits and club challenges fully bilingual and
applies the selected cosmetic theme to their summary surfaces, borders, text and
progress accent. The Arabic layouts remain RTL and the English layouts remain
LTR.

Gift milestones, token and XP amounts, Pasha prices and durations, challenge
identifiers, claim eligibility, economy settlement, permissions and server
authority are unchanged.

Arabic and English widget coverage exercises dark crimson and light emerald
themes at 390×844. R7 captures all twelve locale/theme/surface combinations for
manual visual review.
