# R19 Visual Principles

- Original Warqnaa identity; external products are quality references only.
- Deep navy/glass foundation with controlled cyan/gold accents.
- Arabic RTL is first-class; English LTR remains complete.
- Touch targets and card readability remain protected on compact screens.
- Premium cosmetics are expressive, not pay-to-win.
- Animated effects must remain legible, performant and optional where accessibility requires.
